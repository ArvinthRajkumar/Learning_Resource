Study roadmap

Priority tiers and day-by-day plans that put Essential topics first, add revision checkpoints and timed mocks, and finish with a final revision phase.

7, 14 and 30 day plans~2 hours per day

Short on time? Advanced topics (and then Important ones) drop out of the plan automatically in the shorter plans below. If you are new to programming, start with module 1 even in the short plans.

Priority tiers: what to learn first

Every topic carries a label. The labels reflect general importance for beginner-to-intermediate coding assessments. They are not official Infosys information.

TierMeaningModules
EssentialMaster these first. They appear in most coding tests and are the foundation for everything else.Programming & Problem-Solving Foundations, Time & Space Complexity, Arrays and Lists, Strings, Searching Algorithms, Sorting Algorithms, Hashing, Two Pointers & Sliding Window, Stacks & Queues, Recursion & Backtracking

Principles that matter more than the schedule

  • Understand first, memorise never: if you cannot explain why the solution works, redo the dry run.
  • Re-solve problems a few days later without notes: repetition builds recall.
  • Group problems by pattern (two pointers, sliding window, hashing) so you learn to recognise the technique.
  • After each failed attempt, write down what clue in the statement should have pointed to the right technique.

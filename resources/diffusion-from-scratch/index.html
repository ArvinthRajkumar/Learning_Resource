If you are short on time

  1. The shortcut formula and a worked example, part D3. Everything else in training builds on x_t = sqrt(alpha_bar_t) * x_0 + sqrt(1 - alpha_bar_t) * noise.
  2. What the network predicts and the loss, part D3. It predicts the noise, and the loss is a plain MSE.
  3. The training and sampling loops, part D3. Be able to write both from memory.
  4. The U-Net pieces, part D3. Skip connections, timestep embedding, attention at the bottleneck.
  5. EMA, DDIM and classifier-free guidance, part D5. The three upgrades interviewers ask about most.

Going deeper (parts DX1 to DX7)

After the main guide: the mathematics, samplers, latent diffusion and Stable Diffusion, fine-tuning and control, evaluation and safety, other modalities and training at scale.

DX1 The mathematics of diffusion: DDPM derived step by step

The forward process as a Gaussian chain, the closed form, the reverse posterior, why the network predicts noise, the loss from the variational bound, signal-to-noise ratio, v-prediction and the score and SDE views, each checked numerically.

Advanced

DX2 Samplers and speed: DDIM, ODE solvers, distillation and flow matching

Why sampling is slow, ancestral versus deterministic sampling, DDIM derived, ODE solvers, DPM-Solver, guidance, distillation, consistency models and flow matching, with a working sampler on exactly solvable data.

Advanced

DX3 Latent diffusion and how Stable Diffusion is built

Why diffusion in pixel space is expensive, autoencoders and the VAE, the latent U-Net, text conditioning with CLIP and cross-attention, diffusion transformers, the full text-to-image pipeline and its memory and compute numbers.

Advanced

DX4 Fine-tuning and control: LoRA, DreamBooth, ControlNet, inpainting and editing

Ways to adapt and steer a diffusion model: full fine-tuning, LoRA with parameter counts, textual inversion, DreamBooth, ControlNet, adapters, image-to-image and SDEdit, inpainting, and prompt and seed control.

Advanced

DX5 Evaluation, data, safety and ethics of image generators

How generative image models are measured (FID, Inception Score, precision and recall, CLIPScore, human evaluation), how training data is built, memorisation and privacy, safety filters, watermarking, bias and copyright.

MediumAdvanced

DX6 Beyond images: video, audio, 3D, science, robotics and discrete diffusion

How the diffusion recipe carries over to video, audio and speech, 3D with score distillation, molecules and proteins, robot policies and text, and how diffusion compares with autoregressive and GAN models.

Advanced

DX7 Training lab: a tiny diffusion model, then training at scale

A complete diffusion model small enough to train in pure Python and verify, then the engineering of real training: EMA, mixed precision with loss scaling, gradient clipping, noise offsets, min-SNR weighting, distributed training, compute budgets and debugging.

How to use this guide

  • Read in order. Phase 2 builds each idea on the previous one, so jumping in halfway makes later parts confusing.
  • Click Mark as done at the end of each section. The bars above and the parts menu update.
  • Answers you check in the practice quiz feed the weak-areas box.
  • The guide is written for a laptop with an NVIDIA RTX 2050 (4GB of VRAM), so every size and setting is chosen to fit that. Larger GPUs simply give you more room.
